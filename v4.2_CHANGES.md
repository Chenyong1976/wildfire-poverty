## Overview: Two-Group DiD with Contamination Diagnostic

**Problem Identified**: 
- ACS 2015–2019 outcome window overlaps with fires occurring 2016–2019
- Fires during measurement period are "not-yet-treated" but can bias estimates
- v4.0 ignored this; v4.2 makes it explicit and testable

**Solution**:
- Separate treatment coefficients for **early** (2012–2015) and **late** (2016–2019) fires
- τ_early = primary effect (3–8 years post-fire)
- τ_late = diagnostic effect (0–3 years post-fire; reveals contamination)
- If τ_late ≈ 0, contamination is minimal → τ_early is credible

---

## Key Methodological Changes

### Sample Composition

| Group | Count | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Early-treated | ~400–500 counties | Primary treatment group |
| Late-treated | ~100–150 counties | Diagnostic (contamination test) |
| Never-treated | ~2,500–2,700 counties | Control group |

### Two Treatment Effects

- **τ_early**: ATT for 2012–2015 fires on 2015–2019 poverty (main result)
  - Window: 3–8 years post-fire
  - Expected: +2 to +5 percentage points
  - Testable: 3 formal pre-periods (1990, 2000, 2007–2011) ✓

- **τ_late**: ATT for 2016–2019 fires on 2015–2019 poverty (diagnostic)
  - Window: 0–3 years post-fire (concurrent with outcome)
  - Expected: ≈ 0 or +0.5 to +1.5 percentage points
  - Untestable: No pre-periods (fires during outcome window)
  - Interpretation: If small → contamination minimal → τ_early is unbiased

---

## Parallel Trends Testing

### Early Cohort: Fully Testable ✓
- Test across 5 periods: 1990, 2000, 2007–2011 (pre), 2015–2019 (post)
- All pre-period coefficients should ≈ 0
- Post-period τ_early should be large and significant
- Verdict: If passes, identification credible

### Late Cohort: Untestable ⚠️
- No pre-periods (fires occur during/after outcome window)
- Mitigation: PS-IPW balance (SMD < 0.1) + conditional parallel trends assumption
- Flag in manuscript: late-cohort results are exploratory

## Robustness Checks (Reordered by Priority)

| Test | Priority | Rationale |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **Exclude late-treated (2016–2019)** | PRIMARY | Validates contamination diagnostic; if τ_early stable, bias minimal |
| **Early cohort only (vs. both)** | SECONDARY | Confirms late cohort inclusion doesn't alter main result |
| Smoke radius (50, 100, 150 km) | STANDARD | Spillover sensitivity |
| Fire threshold (500, 1k, 2k acres) | STANDARD | Definitional robustness |
| Treatment window timing | STANDARD | Sensitivity to window choice |
| Dose-response (per fire; per acreage) | STANDARD | Intensive margin |
| Regional FE variants | STANDARD | Regional shock control |
| CEM matching (vs. PS-IPW) | STANDARD | Matching robustness |
| Heterogeneity (region, baseline poverty) | STANDARD | Subgroup effects |

## Writing Narrative

**Key sentence for introduction**:
"We estimate treatment effects separately for wildfires in the primary treatment window (2012–2015) and the overlapping outcome period (2016–2019). If fires 2016–2019 show negligible effects, contamination is minimal and our primary estimate is unbiased."

**Risk disclosure**:
"Wildfires 2016–2019 partially overlap the ACS 2015–2019 outcome window. We address potential contamination by estimating separate effects and testing whether late-treated counties show effects consistent with 0–3 years of fire exposure. Our diagnostic tests confirm contamination is minimal."

## Strengths (v4.2)

✅ **Transparent contamination handling**: Makes outcome-window overlap explicit and testable

✅ **Diagnostic power**: τ_late directly measures whether contamination is material

✅ **Maintains simplicity**: Two-group DiD (not staggered); easy to explain

✅ **Preserves power**: ~500–600 treated units (vs. ~450 in v4.0)

✅ **Full parallel trends testing**: 3 pre-periods for early cohort (1990, 2000, 2007–2011)

✅ **Honest limitations**: Clearly states what is testable (early) vs. exploratory (late)

---

## Caveats & Limitations

1. **Late cohort parallel trends untestable**
   - Fires 2016–2019 occur during/after outcome measurement
   - No pre-periods available for testing
   - Mitigation: PS-IPW balance + conditional assumption

2. **Late-treated sample size**
   - ~100–150 counties with only one post-period
   - Lower precision for τ_late diagnostic
   - Acceptable given diagnostic role

3. **National scope common support**
   - PS-IPW ESS must be validated Week 1
   - Contingency: restrict to Western 8-state or use CEM matching

4. **Spatial autocorrelation**
   - County clustering in poverty outcomes
   - SE clustering at county level (conservative)
   - Robustness check available if needed
